# ASROCOIN Localization

Supported locales:

- English (`en`, source locale)
- Türkçe (`tr`)
- Español (`es`)
- Português do Brasil (`pt_BR`)

The application follows the device locale by default. A manual selection is available under Profile > Settings and is persisted locally.

Locale-aware formatting is used for numbers, percentages and displayed prices. Brand names, usernames, coin names/codes and financial symbols remain untranslated.
